SENware School Pilot

Pilot Overview

SENware is currently being tested through a small-scale pilot programme involving SNAs, school leadership and SEN support staff.

The goal of the pilot is to evaluate whether a simple alert-based communication system can improve awareness of student support needs during the school day.

Pilot Structure

  • 10–11 Special Needs Assistants
  • Vice Principal participation
  • SENCO oversight
  • 1 week live trial
  • Alerts shared through SENware system

Alert Types Being Tested

  • Student absence
  • Medical alerts (e.g. low blood sugar)
  • Behaviour incidents
  • Support needs

Pilot Objectives

  • Test real-time alert sharing
  • Measure staff engagement
  • Evaluate ease of use
  • Assess coordination improvements
