SENware

A communication system designed to help school staff coordinate support for students with additional needs.

The Problem

In many schools, important information about student support needs is shared through informal conversations, WhatsApp groups, or handwritten notes.

This can make it difficult for staff to stay aware of important updates during busy school days.

SENware aims to provide a simple digital system where important alerts and updates can be shared with the right staff when needed.

Key Features

Alert System

Send important updates such as student absence, medical alerts, behavioural incidents, or support needs to relevant staff.

Student Snapshot

Quick overview of important information such as student needs, triggers, and support strategies.

SNA Handover Log

Allows SNAs to leave notes for colleagues or substitutes, helping maintain continuity of care.

Early Pilot Programme

SENware is currently being tested through a small-scale pilot programme involving SNAs and school leadership.

The pilot is designed to evaluate whether a simple alert-based communication system can improve coordination around student supports.
