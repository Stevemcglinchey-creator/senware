SENware

A simple communication system for school staff to coordinate support for students with additional needs.

The Problem

In many schools, student support information is shared through informal conversations, WhatsApp groups, or handwritten notes. Critical updates can be missed during busy school days.

SENware provides a digital system where important alerts and updates are delivered to the right staff in real time.

Key Features

Alert System

Send updates instantly for student absence, medical alerts, behavioural incidents, or support needs to relevant staff.

Student Snapshot

Quick overview of student info, triggers, supports, and medical notes at a glance.

SNA Handover Log

Leave notes for colleagues or substitutes to maintain continuity of care between shifts.
