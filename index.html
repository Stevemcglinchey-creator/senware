SENware

A communication system designed to help schools coordinate support for students with additional needs.

What is SENware?

SENware is a simple digital system designed to help school staff share important information about student support needs in real time.

Alerts System

Send important updates such as absence, medical alerts, behavioural incidents, or support needs to the right staff instantly.

Student Snapshot

Quick overview of key student information such as triggers, supports, and medical needs.

SNA Handover Log

Allows SNAs to leave notes for colleagues or substitutes, ensuring continuity of care.
